Collecting the full set

You know this property. The job is to capture it completely enough that someone who has never been here can build the dashboard from your photos alone.

  1. Work through the house, barns, and fields in whatever order fits the day's work. It does not need to be one trip.
  2. For each item, take one photo of the label up close, and one photo of the whole thing. For big equipment, take a few angles and number them: item1.jpg, item2.jpg, and so on.
  3. Check each label photo is readable before moving on. A retake on the spot is free; a second trip is not.
  4. When the set is complete, drop the photos in the Drive folder above, named for the item each one shows. That is what turns the placeholders on this page into the real dashboard.

Owner's first pass, before the dashboard is trusted

  • Inspect

    Confirm the heating system and fuel

    Type, age, and last service date unknown from the listing. In Maine this is the single most important system. Note it here first.

  • Inspect

    Well and septic: age, capacity, last service

  • Inspect

    Smoke and CO alarms: count and age

    Unlike a Florida house with no gas, a Maine house with combustion heat needs CO alarms as well as smoke alarms. Count them, read the manufacture dates, replace anything over 10 years old.

    Barns and cheese room: condition and equipment

    Walk each outbuilding, note roof, water, electric, and heat, and list the cheese room equipment. This is the farm half of the dashboard and it starts empty.

Maintenance schedule

House and farm, by season. Items marked “confirm” stay until the owner's list replaces them.

Monthly

  • Test smoke and CO alarms
  • AC filter check in cooling season, change when dirty
  • Barn water and feed check (farm routine)

Spring

  • Heating system service after winter
  • Gutters and downspouts: clean, confirm water runs clear of the foundation
  • Exterior walk: caulk, paint, screens, roof look-over
  • Fencing walk: winter damage, gates
  • Well water test confirm

Summer

  • AC tune-up: clear vegetation, clean coils, confirm drain flows
  • AC condensate drain: vinegar pour monthly in season
  • Hay season: fields, equipment, storage
  • Pressure wash siding and driveway confirm

Fall — pre-winter

  • Heating system service before first cold
  • Freeze protection: pipe insulation, heat tape, hose bibs
  • Generator test, if present
  • Snow equipment: fuel, service, staged
  • Chimney / flue inspection
  • Second gutter cleanout after leaves drop
  • Storm shutters / panels: confirm fit and hardware, if present confirm
  • Barn winter prep: water, heat, bedding

Winter

  • Roof snow load watch after storms
  • Driveway and barn access kept clear
  • Well and septic: watch for freeze issues

Yearly

  • Water heater: drain a few gallons to clear sediment
  • Dryer vent duct: disconnect and clean the rigid line
  • Fridge coils: vacuum
  • Dishwasher filter: rinse
  • Shutoff valves: turn main and each fixture valve off and on so they do not seize
  • Washing machine: self-clean cycle every 20 to 30 loads
  • Fire extinguishers: pressure gauge check
  • Septic tank pump-out confirm interval
  • Garage door: sensors, auto-reverse, chain lube
