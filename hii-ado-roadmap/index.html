What the room sees

What Marc sees

CIO, Mission Technologies — from the 9/23 call
  • Transformation mandate, wants a business-facing face of AI inside the division.
  • Absorption is the biggest inhibitor: the org has to get comfortable as modern tooling permeates.
  • Still staffing the people to run modern tech. The who-pays question on bigger moves is open.
  • His lane is hardening, cyber alignment, scale. Downstream of validated work, never upstream of discovery.

What Jen & Bill see

Enterprise PMO, ADO — from the 10/14 call
  • Four-year-old indirect entity built to offload admin from PMs: travel, deliverables, purchasing, portals.
  • Starved: ~2,000 hours of entry-level labor against a $200M effort. The ask list only grows.
  • Each program is its own colony; the division is the federation. Standardization vs. customization, every week.
  • Current playbook is incremental and opportunistic: SaaS, low-code, light AI, one database behind the portals. Eric is the early adopter.

The bet: ADO has real productivity wins on the table, and those are legitimate early victories. But if the motion stops at productivity, the gains get absorbed and nothing changes. Start with the wins, build toward the lab motion: Todd and Eric lead, Max runs the lab, the crowd gets frontier access.

30 — 90 — 180

30 days

Listen. Sit in on rhythms, learn the tools people actually use, map how work gets from conversation to delivery. Come out with two or three candidate pains. Run the first thin slice live.

90 days

Repeat the loop. String wins together. The crowd starts getting frontier access. The business case for the next level of investment forms from evidence.

180 days

The motion is established. Handoffs have named owners. The conversation shifts from "can this work" to "where next."

How success gets tracked

Good

A demo that proves the motion on a real pain. Done fits in one sentence.

Better

Repeated demos that string into a pattern the org trusts.

Best

The org runs the motion itself with Max steering. Validated prototypes flowing into the factories. Prod ROI itself lives in the factories, not in Max's lane.
